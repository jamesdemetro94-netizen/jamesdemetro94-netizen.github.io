Slate Roof Leak Repair on Fort Hill Circle, Staten Island

By Maximum Roofing · Published September 10, 2026

A homeowner on Fort Hill Circle, in the St. George area of Staten Island’s North Shore, contacted Maximum Roofing after experiencing roof leaks. The property had a slate roof, and a section needed repair.

The homeowner explained that finding a roofer who works on slate roofing in Staten Island had been difficult. Maximum Roofing was able to help with the repair the property needed.

A Repair Covering Approximately 104 Square Feet

Our team completed a targeted slate roof repair covering approximately 104 square feet. The work focused on the section that needed attention, addressing the homeowner’s concerns about the leaking roof.

This was a localized repair project. The scope was based on the area requiring work, rather than a replacement of the entire roof.

Helping a Homeowner Find Slate Roofing Service

For this homeowner, finding someone to handle the slate roof was an important part of getting the job moving. They needed a contractor who could take on the repair and complete the work.

Maximum Roofing completed the agreed repair, and the homeowner was happy with the finished job.

This Fort Hill Circle project is an example of our slate roof repair work for homeowners on Staten Island’s North Shore.

What Changes the Cost?

Roof area, slope and shape

Roof surface area differs from interior floor area. Steep sections, valleys, dormers and multiple roof levels change access and the amount of detail work.

Materials and the complete system

Compare the specified covering, underlayment or membrane assembly, insulation where relevant, fastening, edge details and flashing. Review metal roofing, TPO and EPDM according to roof suitability.

Removal and hidden conditions

Existing layers, disposal, wet insulation and damaged decking may change the scope. Ask what the estimate assumes and how concealed damage will be documented and approved after removal.

Flashing, drainage and ventilation

Chimneys, skylights, walls and penetrations need compatible details. Compare provisions for roof edges, drainage and ventilation rather than comparing only the roof covering.

Access and property protection

Building height, delivery space, staging, debris handling and protection of occupied areas affect planning. Identify constraints before the proposal is agreed.

Commercial roof conditions

For commercial roofing, discuss equipment, insulation, drainage, roof traffic and business hours. A residential shingle estimate is not a useful substitute for a commercial flat-roof scope.

Compare Written Estimates on the Same Basis

  1. Confirm the roof areas and materials covered.
  2. Check removal, disposal and cleanup provisions.
  3. Compare decking and insulation assumptions, allowances and exclusions.
  4. Check flashing, drainage, ventilation and roof-edge details.
  5. Ask how additional work is priced and approved.
  6. Review scheduling, access and property protection.
  7. Read the actual material and workmanship warranty terms; do not assume they are identical.

Ask for clarification when one proposal omits an item that another includes. Review our roofing warranty guide and replacement process before deciding.

Could Repair Be the Better Use of Your Budget?

A localized leak on an otherwise serviceable roof may be repairable. Recurring leaks, widespread deterioration or damaged underlying materials can change the decision. Compare the scope and expected benefit of another repair with the proposed replacement after inspection.

Roof Replacement Cost Questions

Can I get an accurate price from my home’s square footage?

No. Interior floor area does not establish roof area, slope, layers, access or material condition. A roof assessment is needed for a reliable property-specific estimate.

Does a replacement estimate include decking repairs?

Check the written scope. Ask which decking work is included, what is assumed to remain, and how concealed deterioration will be priced and approved if discovered.

Is the lowest estimate always the best value?

No. Compare the complete scope, materials, roof details, exclusions, cleanup and written warranty terms. A lower total may cover different work.

What should I have ready for an estimate?

Provide the property address, known roof age and material, leak history, previous repair information and access requirements. Photos taken safely from indoors or the ground can help explain the concern.

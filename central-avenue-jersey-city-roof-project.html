Project story

Commercial Flat-Roof Replacement on Central Avenue, Jersey City, NJ

By Maximum Roofing · Published September 10, 2026

The customer’s problem

A commercial property owner on Central Avenue contacted Maximum Roofing about recurring roof leaks. Water was entering during heavy rain, and the owner wanted a lasting solution rather than another temporary patch.

What we found

Our inspection identified deteriorated roofing material and damaged seams. After reviewing the roof’s condition with the owner, we recommended replacing the affected roofing system.

How we handled the project

Our crew removed the damaged material, prepared the roof surface, and installed a modified-bitumen roofing system. We paid particular attention to seams, roof edges, and flashing around penetrations.

The result

The replacement provided a continuous roofing surface with newly completed seams and flashing details. We reviewed the finished work with the owner and explained the maintenance needed to help protect the roof.

Questions about roofing services

Do you serve Jersey City?

Yes. Maximum Roofing serves Jersey City, including commercial roofing projects such as the Central Avenue replacement described on this page.

Do you work on commercial flat roofs?

Yes. We provide commercial roofing and flat-roof services. An inspection helps determine whether repair, replacement, or another option suits the property.

Are roof coatings available in Jersey City?

We offer roof-coating services for suitable roofs. The existing material, moisture condition, drainage, and required repairs need to be assessed before a coating is recommended. Read about silicone roof coatings.

Does a leaking roof always need replacement?

No. Some leaks can be repaired at a seam, flashing, or another localized defect. Widespread deterioration or recurring problems may call for a larger scope of work. The recommendation depends on an inspection.

What information should I provide when requesting an inspection?

Tell us the property location, building type, approximate roof age if known, where the leak appears, and whether water is currently entering. Photos taken safely from the ground or indoors and records of previous repairs can help.

What should I do while waiting for help with an active leak?

Keep people away from affected areas and electrical hazards, move belongings if it is safe, and contact us to discuss the situation. Avoid climbing onto a wet or damaged roof. See emergency roof-repair information.

What affects the cost of a flat-roof project?

Roof size, access, the roofing system, removal work, the condition of underlying materials, and flashing and drainage details all affect the scope and cost. A written estimate should explain the proposed work and any exclusions.

How long does roofing work take?

The schedule depends on the size and condition of the roof, the work required, weather, and site access. We can discuss a proposed schedule after assessing the property; the timing of one project should not be assumed for another.

What maintenance helps protect a commercial roof?

Keep drainage paths clear and have seams, flashing, penetrations, and roof edges checked periodically and after severe weather. Address small defects before they grow. Learn about roof maintenance.

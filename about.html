About Maximum Roofing

Maximum Roofing provides residential and commercial roofing services from its Staten Island base. This site explains the roofing systems and services offered, shares selected project examples, and helps property owners find the information relevant to their roof.

Services described on the site include roof repair and replacement, commercial and flat roofing, TPO, EPDM, torch-down and silicone-coated systems, slate, metal, gutters, skylights, siding, and related exterior work. The appropriate scope depends on the property, roof assembly, observed condition, access, and project requirements.

How to use this site

Find the service that fits

Start with the page for the roof type or concern, such as roof repair, roof replacement, flat-roof repair, or commercial roofing. Each page describes relevant considerations and next steps.

Review project examples

Read the published Central Avenue project, Leverett Avenue flat-roof project, Fort Hill Circle slate-roof project, and Richmond Hill Road commercial project. These pages describe the information currently available for each job.

Check service coverage

Maximum Roofing lists service coverage on its service areas page. Contact the company to confirm availability for a specific property and project before making plans.

Discuss your property

For an estimate or to ask about a roofing concern, call (732) 395-3945 or email jamesleo@maximumroofing.us. Sharing the property location, roof type, symptoms, and any available proposal documents can help explain the request.

For project decisions, request a written scope and confirm applicable insurance documentation, permits, warranty terms, and qualifications for the work directly with the contractor before authorizing a project.

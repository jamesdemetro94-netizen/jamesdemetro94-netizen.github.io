How to read this. The baseline is five times your entered annual repair estimate, plus estimated disruption costs if provided. Each option is its entered project cost plus five years of entered post-project repair and disruption estimates, minus only optional annual savings you enter when supported by a building-specific estimate or written insurer/broker information. Exposure value and moisture percentage are context fields and are not treated as expected losses. The model does not determine whether a roof is suitable for restoration or predict its life.

Taxes are not modeled. Repair, maintenance, restoration, and replacement may have different accounting and tax treatment depending on the work and circumstances. Ask your tax adviser. Use roof-specific inspection findings and written proposals to confirm scope and suitability.

Transparent method

How the model calculates

Repair baseline

5 × (annual repairs + disruption days per year × cost per disruption day).

Replacement

Initial replacement cost + five years of entered post-project repairs and disruption, less entered annual insurance savings.

Restoration

Initial restoration cost + five years of entered post-project repairs and disruption, less entered annual insurance and energy savings.

Simple payback divides the initial restoration cost by modeled annual operating savings versus the repair baseline. It is undiscounted and excludes financing, taxes, inflation, unplanned work, residual value, and roof service life. Moisture percentage and inventory/equipment exposure are context only, not expected loss calculations.

Planning estimate only. Confirm roof measurements, condition, project scope, code requirements, insurance assumptions, and pricing with qualified professionals and written project documents.

FAQ

Calculator questions

Does the calculator create a roofing quote?

No. It models scenarios from values entered by the visitor and does not inspect the roof or create a contractor proposal.

Are energy or insurance savings guaranteed?

No. They are optional assumptions. Enter them only when supported by project-specific estimates or written information from the relevant professional, utility, insurer, or broker.

Can I share a scenario?

Yes. The share URL encodes inputs. Do not include confidential or sensitive information.

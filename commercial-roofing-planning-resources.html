Planning questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Does every commercial roof replacement require R-30 insulation?

No. The required value depends on jurisdiction, adopted code edition, climate zone, building use, assembly, compliance path, scope, and existing-building provisions. Use the official reference links above and confirm the project with its design professional and AHJ.

Does this page determine code or permit requirements for my building?

No. It is a quick-look reference, not a code opinion or permit approval. The local authority determines which requirements apply to a specific address, filing date, building, and scope.

Can the bid-leveling checklist tell me which proposal is best?

No. It shows whether selected scope items are expressly checked in two proposals. It does not judge design adequacy, contractor qualifications, workmanship, price reasonableness, or warranty enforceability.

Are these project examples typical results or cost guarantees?

No. The cards summarize two individually published project accounts. They do not establish typical performance, savings, price, warranty, or suitability for another building.

Official code and permit references

Checked October 17, 2026. Code editions and local requirements can change; confirm current applicability with the local authority before designing, bidding, or filing.

  • NYC Department of Buildings: Energy Conservation Code · includes the 2025 NYCECC; enforcement began March 30, 2026.
  • NYC DOB NOW · filings, permits, applications, and project status.
  • New York State 2025 Energy Conservation Construction Code · Table C402.1.3 lists above-deck roof insulation R-33ci in climate zones 4 and 5 and R-34ci in zone 6 under the R-value method.
  • New York State Department of State: Building Standards and Codes FAQ · local cities, towns, and villages administer and enforce the codes in their jurisdictions.
  • New Jersey DCA: Current Construction Codes · lists the 2024 I-Codes and ASHRAE 90.1-2022 commercial energy subcode adopted August 17, 2026.
  • ASHRAE Standard 90.1 and addenda · use the standard edition referenced by the current New Jersey subcode; verify its amendments and compliance path.
  • New Jersey DCA: Local code enforcement offices · official information for local and regional construction code enforcement.
  • Connecticut DAS: State Building Code regulations · lists the 2022 CSBC, based on the 2021 I-Codes, including the 2021 IECC.
  • Connecticut DAS: Local building officials · municipal official directory.

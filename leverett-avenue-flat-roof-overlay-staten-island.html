Flat Roof Overlay on Leverett Avenue, Staten Island

By Maximum Roofing · Published September 10, 2026

Maximum Roofing completed a flat roof overlay project on Leverett Avenue, in the South Shore region of Staten Island.

Video from the project

Open the video directly · 7-second clip

Assessing the Existing Roof

The homeowner had previously applied a silicone coating to help extend the life of the existing roof. The coating served its purpose for a while, and the owner initially wanted to apply another coat.

After assessing the roof’s condition, our team determined that it had deteriorated beyond what another silicone application could reasonably address. We recommended a new torch-down roofing system.

Completing the Torch-Down Overlay

Our team installed the new torch-down roofing layer over the existing flat roof, completing the overlay. The homeowner was happy with the finished work.

This Leverett Avenue project highlights why evaluating the condition of an existing roof is an important step before deciding between another coating and new roofing.
